What is the difference between a standard and a deep clean?

A standard clean keeps a home that is already in decent shape fresh: kitchen, bathrooms, dusting and floors. A deep clean is the full reset for homes that need it: baseboards, doors, frames and switches, vents and fans, plus the built-up grime in the kitchen and bathrooms.
