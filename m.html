FAQ

Good to know.

Do we bring supplies?

Yes. Our crew brings everything needed for the clean.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. If you won't be there, just leave us a key, a door code, or a lockbox code, whatever works for you, and our crew will let themselves in.

How do I pay?

We save a card on file when you book and only charge it after the job is done.

What if I need to cancel?

Just give us at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice have a $40 fee.

What if something gets missed?

Tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and re-clean it at no charge.
