Our Process

SIX STEPS.

01

CONSULTATION

Tell us what you want your home to look like. We'll discuss your preferred style, colors, areas you want decorated, and any additional features such as wreaths, bushes, walkways, columns, or trees.

02

MEASURE & DESIGN

We measure your property and build a lighting plan designed around the architecture of your home. Our team determines the amount of lighting, materials, power routing, and installation requirements before your scheduled installation.

03

CUSTOM PREPARATION

Your lighting system is prepared specifically for your home before or during installation. Strands are custom-cut and fitted to create clean lines without excessive bulbs or dangling wire.

04

PROFESSIONAL INSTALLATION

Our crew installs your lighting system and makes sure everything is properly positioned, powered, and tested before leaving.

05

SEASON-LONG SUPPORT

If something needs attention during the season, contact Novo's and we'll help get your display back looking right.

06

TAKEDOWN & STORAGE

After the holidays, we return to remove your display, organize your lighting system, and store it for the next season.
